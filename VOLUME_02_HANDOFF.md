# ANDREW THARWAT — VOLUME 02

**Review concept:** A genuinely personal editorial folio, not an agency homepage.

## Removed from previous concept
- Sci-fi rings, glowing orbs, faux radio waves
- Dark generic cards and "tech startup" layouts
- Empty promotional storytelling and invented counts

## Creative direction
Typography and the real Andrew portrait lead. Printed-volume navigation (four chapters), carefully designed project typographic plates, cool graphite + warm paper palette, one restrained copper accent. Each design choice refers to real work: AT Studio problem→solution; ATS AIR listener/library/broadcast; ROZY catalog/variants; Move Now request→owner→follow-up; HSE Learning content→decision→gap→action; QR/docs/evidence.

## Editorial accuracy
AT Studio is presented as founder's studio. ATS AIR is a product, not a claimed employment contract. ROZY and Move Now are project experiences; no unproven traffic or revenue. HSE Training is labeled as a pilot. Document tools are described as workflows, not a falsely unified commercial system. Public site/project links are used; no restricted source plans or employee data.
